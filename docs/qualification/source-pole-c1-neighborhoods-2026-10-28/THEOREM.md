# C1 owned source-star graphs without a common Hessian

The factory borrows one private stratified boundary certificate and the exact
same admitted body. A tangent graph pole stratum (or its stronger curvature
form) supplies a complete one-sheeted source-star graph over an open projected
disk, punctured rank-two charts and one common uniform limiting tangent plane.
Neither caller flags nor a list of sampled normal vectors establishes these
premises.

For every edge of the complete native pole link, the factory requires:

1. A non-C0 declared class. Authored sharpness cannot be silently upgraded.
2. A private polar normal or curvature token covering the full original closed
   carrier, including a continuous nonzero factored-normal limit at its pole.
3. Fresh exact original graph-gradient identities. Original surface weights,
   natural UV-line weights and forward projective carrier weights enter the
   exact predicate; no rounded composition or extracted derivatives enter.

The predicate checks every coefficient of both rational gradient identities.
The projected Jacobian polynomial must not be identically zero. Consequently
identities hold on a dense parameter locus. Existing continuous nonzero normal
coverage extends their tangent-plane equality through isolated projection
zeros; the private transverse graph charts provide actual graph gradients in
the local disk. At every non-pole meridian the incident C1 graph pieces have
identical first derivatives. Finite piecewise gluing gives a C1 graph on the
punctured projected disk. The common uniform plane limit and a separated
projection component then imply a common uniform graph-gradient limit at the
pole, yielding a C1 graph on the entire disk.

All vertices, native link edges and source identities share explicit aggregate
incidence and exact-work budgets. Every attempted identity spends work,
including failed attempts. No partial atlas is returned. Reuse is local to the
same immutable body, exact edge and graph axis in one factory call.

The first-order predicate has a separate opaque native token: it cannot be
used as a second-order meridian certificate. A jump of Hessians may coexist
with exact G1. C2 at poles still needs the independent common quadratic jets
and exact full G2 coverage required by the C2 factory.

This theorem concerns local original source-star neighborhoods. It does not
claim that all portions of the body over the projected disk belong to that
star, that the original collapsed parameterization is regular at its pole,
that the entire body is C1, or that arbitrary ideal sweep laws have been bound
to the retained shell. Sharp rims remain C0 and topology comes from the private
admitted source body, not from this smoothness composition in isolation.
