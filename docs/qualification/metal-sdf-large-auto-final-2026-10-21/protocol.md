# Natural-idle SDF qualification — protocol fixed before A/A and A/B

Apple M4 Max / Metal, release + LTO. Frozen executable copied outside Cargo's target outputs; SHA-256 checked before/after every window. Runner copy and source snapshots (SDF, GPU/compute runtime, geometry/math dependencies) are retained. No signals are sent to observed work. Only a runner-created fixture may be terminated on timeout or cancellation.

Eight full public-workflow cases: GPU sphere 64³; Auto sphere 64³ (CPU); Auto tetrahedron 48³ (CPU), 49³ and 64³ (GPU); Auto closed bipyramids with 84/86 triangles at 32³; 84→86→84 triangle sequence at 32³. The padded GPU input arena sizes are 4048/4120 bytes, bracketing 4096. Independent-buffer fallback stays cached after shrink. CPU branches must allocate no GPU buffers and exactly match explicit CPU. Every case asserts nonempty output and exact coordinate-bit/index parity for A, B and A/A.

A differs from B only by disabling the SDF small-input arena. Shaders, submission, waits, readback and polygon extraction are identical. Every timed repetition calls `polygonize_accelerated` and drops the full result. Transition samples force the cache empty outside each timer, then time all three complete public calls, including initial allocation and growth. Pipelines remain retained. Revised sampling: each mesh/cross sample averages 32 workflows (32 three-call sequences for cross); other cases average eight. Warm-up is 3000 ms, with 48 alternating ABBA/BAAB blocks. All complete-workflow timers and numerical acceptance gates are unchanged.

Admission: 15 seconds without running compiler/test/explicit benchmark competitors, five seconds AGX device utilization ≤10%, and five-second mean total CPU idle ≥80%. Observation only; waiting has no deadline. Running/stopped identity is read, never modified. While a fixture runs, sample competitor identities and utilization every 0.5 seconds. Any observed competitor or GPU utilization >10% one second after fixture exit rejects that entire window; raw data stay preserved. Rejected environmental windows wait and retry. A clean numerical control failure ends the series rather than selecting favorable controls.

Three A/A-only windows of EVERY case must pass before A/B. Each A/B window also includes A/A, again three windows per case. A/A median ratio must be within [0.97,1.03], with relative 95% interval width ≤1.06. Pair log-ratios, bootstrap their median with 10,000 fixed-seed resamples. A new gain requires all three A/B lower 95% bounds >1 and stable controls; a reproducible regression requires all three upper bounds <1. Identical CPU/fallback paths are negative controls and cannot establish an optimization. An interval crossing one establishes neither gain nor regression.

Only accept a production change when the full-path evidence supports it. Retain the previously qualified small-workload path unless new evidence establishes a regression. No Auto threshold changes without separate paired evidence. Historical interrupted/contaminated runs in sibling directories are excluded from this series.
