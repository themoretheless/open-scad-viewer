# Added sufficient predicates

All arithmetic below is rational, on the exact binary64-authored coefficients.
No tolerance closes a crack and no sampled equality replaces a source identity.
The existing atlas local-disk, link-Jordan and nonincident-pair checks remain
mandatory after every chart change. This extends the Rust checker, not a proof
assistant formalization.

## Collapsed parameter boundaries

Rotate the tensor square, preserving orientation, until a collapsed side is
v=0. For every homogeneous power coefficient of u^i v^j require j>=i whenever
the coefficient is nonzero. Then x=v, y=u*v gives an exact triangular polynomial
homogeneous map, on 0<=y<=x<=1. For v>0 this is an invertible parameter change;
v=0 is the single boundary point already proved by exact rational equality.
The homogeneous triangular weights must be strictly positive, so no hidden
pole or undefined limit is admitted. Reverse the triangular orientation to
match the original UV orientation. The usual global embedding checks prove
that the image, including the collapsed boundary, is a valid disk.

This does not handle every rational/conical singular chart. A failed
factorization leaves the original collapsed simplex rejected; it is not skipped.

## Nonlinear seam clocks

For two equally oriented edge maps A and B, find their first nonzero endpoint
Taylor coefficient. Their orders must agree. The ratio of these jets determines
r^k; require an exactly checked positive rational kth root r. Independently
check the entire identity B(t)=A(r*t/(1+(r-1)*t)) by homogeneous Bernstein scaling
and rational cross multiplication. Endpoint or tangent agreement alone is not
a witness. Common homogeneous factors and different degrees are allowed.

For each triangle, unknown positive vertex scales s0,s1,s2 define a projective
barycentric map. On the edge a->b its parameter clock is s_b/s_a. All paired
edge-clock equations are solved together by exact multiplicative Gaussian
elimination. Positive rational powers/roots, all cycles and all resulting edge
identities must pass. Free scales are one; a poor sufficient projection may
still refuse a valid complex. Whole-chart projective changes are homeomorphisms
of the closed triangle, hence preserve the source patch images and their union.
The existing homotopy proof then runs on the synchronized charts.

Budgets: at most128 transported triangles, exact root/exponent orders at most64,
and8192 coefficient bits, in addition to the existing global predicate budget.
General non-projective correspondences remain unproved.

## Curvilinear triangular trims

One outer three-edge UV wire, without holes, is represented by its complete
rational Bernstein segments. A positive homogeneous linear factor may adjust
endpoint gauges without changing the curve or its clock. Prefer gauges from
higher-degree curves, to avoid unnecessary degree growth. Fill the triangular
control net with these exact boundary controls and positive weighted barycentric
interior controls.

Require the UV homotopy to its corner triangle to have a positive projected
Jacobian, monotone edges and an exact Jordan boundary throughout. The same disk
degree argument as the parent atlas gives a homeomorphism onto the region bounded
by the original trim. This is an embedded parameter disk; no overlapping signed
radial fans or convexification substitute for it.

All UV controls must remain inside one tensor knot cell. Compose the original
surface exactly with this rational triangular UV map. Dyadic chart subdivisions
are restrictions of that same map; all source seams and the complete resulting
surface embedding are checked again. General multi-edge/holed UV triangulations
and unresolved internal source-knot crossings remain unproved.

## Authority and delivery

This certifies the represented oriented source face images. It does not promote
an inconsistent stored edge representation to editable CAD geometry. Normal model
validation is retained before atlas building. The sphere fixture uses exact
rational circular clocks and radius3 with exactly representable homogeneous
surface coefficients; it is deliberately distinct from the default constructor's
sqrt(1/2)-weighted approximate circular trim.

The standalone source certificate can use these maps. A Minkowski source receipt
still additionally binds the proved endpoint to the actual nominal triangle
chain, and the enclosure's seam preflight is retained. A source certificate alone
never proves topology of the complete Minkowski sum. Shared WASM/dist are not
promoted by this qualification.
