# Exact first-order regular carriers and complete mixed G1/G2 collections

## First-order proof

Only sealed original source carriers supply positional correspondence. Fresh
exact graph-gradient identities are formed from the original surface controls,
weights, natural UV-line weights and original projective carrier maps. The
projected Jacobian polynomial must not vanish identically on either side.
This proves equality of tangent planes on a dense locus of the original
meridian. Neither sampled normal agreement nor an angular tolerance supplies
that equality; Hessian identity is deliberately not required.

Independent native normal rectangles cover the ENTIRE closed retained carrier.
A separated component of each original cross-product normal proves its rank
two on every accepted rectangle. Original positive-weight single-Bezier source
charts and supported projective natural meridians are smooth where regular.
Their continuous normal-plane fields therefore extend the exact equality
through isolated projection zeros and to the retained endpoints. A singular
original endpoint does not satisfy this premise and cannot be cropped away.
This proves exact G1 tangent-plane compatibility of the owned carrier.

Every attempted carrier cell and UV-curve span is charged. Exact layout
admission establishes a single original Bezier span per surface. Each normal
visit is therefore prepaid BEFORE native arithmetic, so even numeric failures
retain their cost. Unresolved closed cells remain pending. Exhaustion or
precision failure returns no partial seam. No orientation agreement, curvature,
Hessian compatibility, source-star graph or body embedding is inferred from
this first-order primitive alone.

## Complete mixed collection

The collection factory borrows one immutable private complete body collection.
Its exact positional ownership, body geometry, topology and declared classes
cannot be supplied by caller-written reports. All polar-record and edge-class
visits share one incidence ledger. Every non-C0 request must succeed at its
UNCHANGED order:

* Regular G1: fresh exact original gradient identity plus full regular normal
  coverage; no Hessian or curvature premise is used.
* Polar G1: fresh exact original gradient identity plus that same collection's
  privately retained full continuous factored-normal coverage and pole limits.
  This asserts tangent-plane compatibility along its closed carrier, not a C1
  neighborhood of the whole pole star.
* Regular G2: fresh exact original gradient/Hessian identity plus independent
  native regular shape coverage and ONE coherent normal/tensor orientation.
* Polar G2: only this collection's already exact private polar G2 token can be
  reused. A tolerance-only G2 token refuses; it is never downgraded to G1.

Fresh exact work, carrier queries, UV-curve spans and original normal/shape
visits are aggregate across all regular and polar edges and both orders. A
reused immutable native token retains its existing proof and is recorded as
reuse, not newly computed credit. A prefix, one failed edge or exhausted work
never yields a complete collection token. C0 stays C0, and G1 is not silently
promoted to G2 even when that geometry also happens to be G2.

## Scope

These are exact seam guarantees on the original REPRESENTED body. They do not
bind an arbitrary ideal loft/sweep law to that representation. Whole-body C1/C2
requires independently proved face interiors and all applicable source-star
neighborhoods. Filled cap topology, ideal-source regularity, global ideal-body
embedding, general frame families and closed seam quotients remain separate
obligations. Unsupported original layouts/maps and singular or unproved cases
must refuse rather than authorize rounded proxies or cropped domains.
