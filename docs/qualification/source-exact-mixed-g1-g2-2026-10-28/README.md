# Exact original mixed G1/G2 seam qualification

`qualify_tangency_exact` proves original graph-gradient identities and complete
regular native normal coverage without requiring matching Hessians or shape
tensors. `qualify_exact_smooth_collection` composes every requested G1/G2
carrier of one immutable private source-body collection under shared budgets.
C0 and G1 classes retain their original order. See THEOREM.md for proof scope.

A closed curved prism has bottom-profile branches `y=x*x` and `y=2*x*x`.
Their join is exactly G1 with a genuine curvature jump. Its original body
passes native embedded-geometry admission and has certified volume interval
`[20.99999999999988, 21.00000000000015]`, containing 21. The owned exact G1
collection uses 15 incidence steps, 3828 exact operations, one carrier cell
and two original normal spans; its G2 attempt refuses the unequal Hessians.

Additional coverage includes eight nonzero-curvature-jump affine-frame/weight
cases, a regular seam crossing a projection zero, singular endpoints despite
dense first-order identities, genuine derivative jumps, tight/one-short work
limits, a same-body collection with one G1 and two G2 seams, exact polar G1
on folded quadratic caps, same-collection exact polar G2 reuse and rejection
of tolerance-only polar G2 upgrades. Rounded represented sphere-like caps must
continue to refuse exact G1 when their original gradients actually differ.
Read verification.json and the frozen logs for completed results.

This milestone concerns original REPRESENTED seam geometry. It does not
complete global embedding/regularity/topology for arbitrary ideal loft/sweep
laws, general frame/cap families, face interiors or closed seam quotients.
Whole pole neighborhoods require their separate graph certificates. Native
qualification is targeted; it is not the full workspace or semantic G1 matrix,
a WASM/browser rebuild or remote CI. Architecture audit remains a delivery
gate and its baseline is not relaxed.

Frozen qualification: 21 native command groups and 136 passing Rust tests;
10 audit-tool tests pass. All Rust input hashes are unchanged. The mixed
regular collection uses 21 steps, 7814 exact operations, 5 carrier cells and
10 normal/shape spans. Eight polar G1 carriers use 28 steps and 62512 fresh
exact operations while retaining their original native limit coverage.
Architecture audit still emits 82 diagnostic lines.
