# Original classified closed material boundary

## Authority

`certify_original_classified_closed_material` takes the actual immutable original
sweep, complete loop partition, original `LoftCap` surface/UV contours and a class
for every exact source Bezier span. It accepts no successful report or external
proof token. Its private successful owner retains the exact original material
borrow and a freshly constructed original classified volume owner borrowing that
same material surface and sweep.

The source material stage freshly:

1. Validates original XYZ/UV data and complete loop counts; a closed source is
   required. Exactly decomposes every original UV contour and world profile.
2. Proves a simple outer UV contour, pairwise separated opposite-winding holes,
   their containment inside the outer and absence of nested holes. Undecided
   simplicity, separation, winding or containment gives no successful object.
3. Proves every original profile regular and exactly equal, with the forward affine parameter registration of each exact original
   span, to the image of its corresponding UV contour on
   the actual original material surface. Every span and every loop is covered.
4. Inspects every extracted UV Bezier control row. Positive rational hulls bound
   the whole boundary inside the natural carrier rectangle. The bounded simple
   material region is then inside that convex rectangle too. An unresolved
   carrier domain or exhausted budget refuses; periodic extension or mere image
   equality supplies no alternative authority.

The native classified stage freshly proves the complete original filled-map
embedding, regularity and quotient topology, complete carrier C1/C2 continuity,
actual periodic motion/frame C1/C2, every source trace regular, exact position
closure and each declared positive G1/G2 geometric jet. C0 remains C0 at authored
corners. The old uniform closed smooth-volume API retains its original behavior.

These facts jointly apply to the actual material subset: restriction of the
proved global filled embedding to the valid, bound material domain preserves
its topology and forbids self-intersection. Profile regularity plus valid carrier
binding and the full-rank volume chart give regular swept material walls. The
common Ck motion transports each exact positive profile jet to the corresponding
longitudinal wall join. The periodic Ck chart gives the closed traversal seam
away from declared sharp joins. Opposite UV winding retains the outer/hole roles.

This original-geometry certificate does not construct or certify a retained
BRep Solid or its face orientation, approximation error, tessellation or STEP
representation. The reference filled carrier of a closed sweep is not a physical
end cap. Existing actual-Solid owners remain separate.

## Work accounting and refusal

Original UV/profile decomposition products, original profile regularity cells,
exact profile/carrier binding work, carrier-domain hull rows, and all native
classified-volume cells/exact-work/products share one aggregate budget. The
native stage receives only the remaining allowance. Native profile visits and
joins share their explicit steps allowance. UV simplicity, separation, winding
and containment retain a separate explicit `MaterialLimits` ledger, including
pair, cell and domain-cell counts in the returned material report. Neither
ledger is reset between its stages. Refusals return evidence without a successful
material owner. Immutable source coefficients and derivatives are preserved.

## Native regression scope

The new source fixtures cover a spatial closed path with authored moving frame
and positive arc clock, a split polygon with sharp C0 corners and G2 straight
joins, an additional oppositely oriented hole, a nonplanar filled carrier, and
rational G1/G2 joins. Mutations exercise UV contour mismatch, source carrier
replacement, wrong hole winding, missing hole ownership, unsupported G2 upgrades
and exhausted aggregate/material work. A straight UV image with a changed
rational clock is refused by the exact span-registration route; a separate
nonlinear image registration is not inferred from matching endpoints. Final executed catalogs and frozen-source
hashes are authoritative for which checks passed.

General contact/polar regimes, genuinely different carrier registrations and
other non-affine loft families retain their own unproved conditions and refusal
paths. This packet makes no whole-product, WASM/browser/STEP or remote CI claim.
