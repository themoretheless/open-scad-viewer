# Immutable original-span reuse

Linear injectivity currently rebuilds the same original homogeneous span net on every DFS subcell. The private candidate retains one span per immutable borrowed surface, evicts before changing span, and prepares translated value/dv/du nets and their Bernstein convex hulls. Every query preserves original interval normalization, Bernstein evaluation order, hull intersection and rational quotient recurrence. Restrict-then-differentiate remains independent and unchanged. Station relative/position sources have separate caches tied to their immutable source borrows.

Logical cell/depth/work charges and original domains remain unchanged. No proof is reused across a source mutation or another surface. Storage is bounded by the current source span rather than all searched cells/spans. The uncached full-order and first-order routines remain independent test oracles. Positive certification still requires the same interval monotonicity inequalities and global cover.
