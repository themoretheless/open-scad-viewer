# Exact identity of original normalized filled carriers

The source volume is `F(u,v,t) = pose(t)((1-t) C0(u,v) + t C1(u,v))`,
where each original cap is evaluated in its own **normalized** UV domain.
Literal equality of the stored `Surface` structures is sufficient but was
unnecessarily restrictive for both the differential enclosure and the periodic
original-source quotient. This change proves a broader exact identity.

## Original-input premises

Both carriers are validated XYZ tensor surfaces with positive original weights,
the same U/V degrees and rectangular net dimensions, and no periodic flags.
Corresponding original Cartesian controls must agree exactly. In each direction,
all original knots, including exterior and repeated knots, must satisfy the same
positive affine correspondence determined by the two original natural-domain
endpoints. Finally, one common positive factor must relate **all** original
weights. Separate per-row/per-column factors are not sufficient.

Knot correspondence is proved by exact zero orientation of the original paired
endpoint/knots. Weight correspondence is proved by exact zero orientation of
`(0,0)`, `(wa[0],wb[0])`, and every original `(wa[i],wb[i])`. Because validated
natural domains have positive lengths and weights are positive, these exact
identities establish the required positive factors without computing rounded
ratios. Only binary64 bits read from the original immutable surfaces enter the
predicate arena. A nonzero, unresolved or resource-limited predicate supplies no
identity. Equal literal surfaces retain their pre-existing zero-work route.

## Map and differential consequence

Positive affine knot correspondence preserves the complete B-spline basis
recursion after normalizing each domain. Repeated-knot support and one-sided
limits are preserved, so this argument applies to multispan tensors as well as
single Bezier patches. Equal Cartesian controls and proportional homogeneous
weights therefore make the rational numerator and denominator differ by the
same positive scalar. Their quotient is the same function on the full natural
normalized rectangle, and every existing normalized derivative agrees.

In particular, `C1-C0` and its normalized U/V derivatives are identically zero;
`(1-t)C0+tC1` is the single original carrier, with zero carrier time derivative.
The native original-jet owner still computes actual bounds and uses each
original knot-cell/domain width for the chain rule. Raw authored derivatives are
neither overwritten nor confused with normalized derivatives. The independent
regression formula checks rational values and first/second derivatives in both
nonunit domains, including endpoints.

## Ownership, work and global use

The private identity object borrows the exact two immutable original surfaces;
its use checks both references. It is created once per volume certificate and
cannot be supplied by a caller. The full original net/knot inspection is charged
before predicate construction, with a 100,000-cell source-stage ceiling and a
bounded predicate arena. Every predicate consumes the same aggregate exact-work
ledger as the subsequent original frame and global body proof. Failed trials do
not reset either allowance. An open source may still prove its actual moving
carrier family after an unsuccessful identity trial using the remaining work.

This identity is used by fixed-metric differential enclosures, exhaustive global
pair charts, intrinsic original tube premises, and exact periodic source
ownership. It supplies no regularity, injection, trim validity, frame smoothness
or topology guarantee by itself. Those existing native owners are still run;
closed sources additionally need their exact periodic law/frame premises and a
complete wrapped pair cover. Actual different endpoint caps must not acquire a

correspondence and binds the source profile loops and retained filled caps.

retained-face regularity and material-volume checks. No geometric computation is
