# Transverse original cap/wall spans

The original capped material owner now exposes a typed transverse span for
each exact binding at either cap. Each span borrows the existing immutable
source address, including the endpoint, ring, profile and trim span. The
compatibility classification is C0. This proof describes the original
boundary and its two tangent planes.

For the original volume map F, the cap has tangent plane spanned by F_u
and F_v. Along a regular trim gamma, the wall tangent plane is spanned by
the derivative of F along gamma and by F_t. The first vector is nonzero
because the trim matches a regular original profile. At a full-rank volume
chart F_t is outside the cap plane. This gives an exact shared edge with
distinct tangent planes at every original span and from each incident span
at a sharp trim vertex. C0 is therefore forced on this regular product
boundary. The result derives directly from the existing typed volume,
material and normalized trace witnesses and performs no new interval or
sampling work.

The hollow fixture uses distinct filled cap interiors, differing rational
weights and raw UV domains, and matched traces on both its outer and inner
rings. The native test matches every transverse span back to its exact
source binding. An independent rational oracle checks the native-emitted
fixture and derives determinant 2 for the two cap derivatives and the
wall traversal derivative along the entire extent of all twenty spans.

Smooth cap/wall transitions with different boundary charts need their own
regularity, joining and embedding proofs. The transverse span certificate
applies to the ideal product boundary.
