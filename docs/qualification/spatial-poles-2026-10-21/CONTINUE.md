# Next boundaries

This milestone expands sufficient exact source-atlas predicates in Rust.
See README.md and manifest.json for the final native evidence.

Still outside the proved classes:
- Genuine nonsmooth conical poles and poles without a proved triangular projection.
- Rational/non-polynomial graph lifts and unresolved inverse branches.
- Seam inverse observables above quadratic degree or nullspace combinations not proposed here.
- Multiple unpartitioned knot cells, insufficient sign predicates and finite arithmetic/work budgets.

Do not admit zero weights, remove singular cells or treat sample agreement as an identity.
New source-atlas support must not silently change the frozen legacy v4 enclosure recipe.
WASM/browser/host/dist qualification is separate from native Rust qualification.
