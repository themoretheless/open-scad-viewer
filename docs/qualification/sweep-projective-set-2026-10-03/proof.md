# Sufficient constant projective strip jets

For the same along-seam basis and positive weights, normalize the two homogeneous boundaries by opposite first boundary weights. Require the resulting boundary H to agree coefficientwise. Let A1,A2 and B1,B2 be inward derivatives in normalized cross parameters. For an authored positive constant s, prove exactly:

B1+s A1=lambda H

B2-s² A2=mu H+nu A1

The constants lambda,mu,nu are shared by every along coefficient. The first identity is checked by cross multiplication using positive H.w. The second uses a nonzero two-coordinate determinant of H and A1, then checks every remaining coefficient. Failure to find a determinant, arithmetic exhaustion or insufficient work does not certify continuity.

Applying the rational quotient gives b1=-s a1 and b2=s² a2+(nu+2s lambda)a1. These are the transverse first and second jets of a constant second-order reparameterization. Differentiating the coefficient identities along the shared basis also supplies the mixed-jet relation. Independent interval regularity remains a prerequisite. For multispan along bases, knot multiplicities must support the requested order.

All equations use exact represented authored scalars. The common arithmetic denominator and power-of-two normalization are carried by an explicit representation of one; every product is homogeneous in that unit. No floating-point division or epsilon proves equality.

This is sufficient geometric G1/G2 for this restricted reparameterization. It does not certify arbitrary tangential shear or along-dependent reparameterizations. A refusal does not prove a geometric discontinuity. It does not establish global embedding, cap smoothness, solid validity or volume. Sharp miter corners are not promoted to G1/G2.

## Exact smoothness of linear along spans

A shared clamped degree-one along basis with internal knots is admitted only after an additional exact proof on every original homogeneous cross-layer coefficient. For each internal row i, compare adjacent homogeneous divided differences by cross multiplication with the original positive knot span widths. Equality on every layer means the entire homogeneous surface has one common along derivative, and all higher along derivatives vanish. With positive weights its rational quotient is smooth at those knots. Spanwise interval normal bounds then independently certify seam regularity. This special proof is not applied to the old strict homogeneous auditor and cannot promote a kink or unsupported higher-degree repeated knot.

For a set, every declared seam consumes one shared exact-work budget; any refusal clears the set's certified order. An empty declaration set never certifies smoothness. The caller owns completeness of adjacency declarations; the result concerns that declared set, not all faces or caps of a body.
