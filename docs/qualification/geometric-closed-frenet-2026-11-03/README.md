# Closed Frenet sweeps with unequal source speeds

A closed ArcLength Frenet source can now establish C1 or C2 frame continuity
from original position identities and exact first two or three physical-arc
derivatives of the unit tangent, respectively. The proof covers internal
knots and the periodic join. The existing exact Cartesian derivative route
is tried first. The geometric route checks coorientation, exact derivative
identities, the original twist joins, and nonzero speed and curvature on
the entire source. Since the normal is the normalized derivative of the
unit tangent with respect to arc length, a C3 unit tangent gives a C2
Frenet triad.

The global body proof independently covers time pairs and proves nonsingular
local charts over the full filled carrier. Within one original Bézier span,
an exact change in the binary exponents of the weights represents a positive
Möbius change of parameter. Shared endpoint weights are matched by a common
homogeneous factor on the following span. The oriented path in normalized
physical arc length is invariant, along with its Frenet frame, scale,
twist and affine laws. The atlas uses this identity for eligible closed
ArcLength Frenet paths after testing the seams of the exact input. The
public certificate remains bound to the original sweep and material. The
conditioning step retains the original weights if the span layout or
binary64 exponent range prevents exact storage.

Arc bounds account for the overlap between the prefix integral and the total
length. The one-sided frame and inverse-clock derivative bounds are composed
before hulling at a speed jump. The spatial atlas caches its complete UV
enclosure and uses a reference frame at the midpoint of accumulated angular
variation. Each chart contains the entire derivative family along the
connected lift, including the wrapped seam.

The independent oracle reads the native source records and checks the
positive Möbius identity using rational polynomial composition. It computes
physical-arc tangent derivatives through order three by formal series
composition and inversion. The checked controls include unequal endpoint
speeds, an internal speed jump, a one-ULP fourth-position-jet perturbation,
and complete positive reference curvature covers.

The native atlas certifies both alternate clocks with variable scale, twist,
affine axes and center laws on planar and warped carriers. The two multi-span
atlas cases use 35,061 and 38,637 cells. The public BRep matrix passes all 16
combinations of clock, carrier and material with or without a hole. It verifies
source pointer ownership, exact trim bindings, winding, closed C2 traversal,
G2 profile joins, and regularity, embedding and topology of the volume.
All three arc cases with a warped carrier and hole replay their exact budgets,
refuse each one-less budget, and reject a one-ULP trim change. An explicitly
sharp material ring retains C0 at every corner and refuses a G1 claim there.

The heaviest public case used 43,984 cells, 246,741 units of exact work and
104 products. The independent oracle checks both alternate-clock material
records for eight exact lifted bindings each. The complete NURBS unit suite
passed 2,276 tests with one ignored. All 159 exact predicate tests passed.

Qualification covers original closed ArcLength Frenet sources with the
reported frame and material conditions. General corrected Frenet, arbitrary
authored fields and unproved cap/wall G1 or G2 joins require additional
certificates. This packet qualifies native operations.
