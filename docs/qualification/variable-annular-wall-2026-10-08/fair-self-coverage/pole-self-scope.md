# Factored pole self-wall experiment

Four authored variable annular prototypes, endpoint radii 0.5/1.25 mm in both orders, two orientations. Only faces 0 and 10 are selected. Threshold 4.74999 mm and angular threshold 1e-4; this differs from the 5.99 mm source Body acceptance fixture.

At 512 cells and 4096 spans per selected face, all eight runs remain unqualified. Factored angular exclusions confirm individual cells only. Span exhaustion occurs before the cell cap. Exact normal and differential preparation are separately counted in the original report. No global wall or self-intersection certificate is claimed.

A larger bounded run is pending. These facts are not yet wired to the Body self-wall adapter.
