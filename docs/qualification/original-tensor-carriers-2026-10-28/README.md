# Exact original tensor carriers with different degrees and nets

Rust now proves equality of complete original normalized rational B-spline cap
maps across different degrees, Cartesian control nets, rational gauges and knot
partitions. The proof covers the full common knot rectangles with exact
homogeneous arithmetic; it does not compare sampled points or admit a rounded
extracted/elevated surface as source authority. See THEOREM.md.

A common positive gauge is retained for every active blossom and the entire
other-axis net. Independent row normalization is forbidden. Counterexamples
include separately identical row curves whose changed relative gauges alter the
whole tensor, matching boundaries with an interior-only change, different
normalized parameterizations of the same geometric image, invalid/disconnected
bases, foreign leaves, cancelled/deadline-limited trials and insufficient work.

The native NURBS adapter reads both original surfaces and retains one private
borrowed identity for those exact references. Failed trials share the original
body's cell and exact-work ledger. Fixed-metric, exhaustive original-pair,
intrinsic tube and closed-quotient owners keep their separate global premises.

and second jets are checked independently without changing original derivatives.

The closed regression combines a degree-one multispan cap and a degree-two
rational cap with different domains and nonconstant weights. It freshly proves
its spatial authored frame and source boundary compatibility at order two,
and completes the original filled-body pair cover, including the periodic seam.

caps to the original profile loops and retained boundary, and proves retained

## Remaining scope

This milestone extends the actual native original-body certificate; it does not
complete the entire goal. The tensor comparison admits degrees 1..8 and at most
64 controls per direction under the explicit exact arithmetic budget/envelope.
Higher or unsupported layouts and unproved arithmetic still refuse this route.
Other global law/frame/cap combinations, nonlinear closed gluing maps, all
filled-boundary G1/G2 modes and complete semantic qualification remain open.
Authored C0 joins are not promoted. No geometry moved to TypeScript.

The targeted native run is not a full workspace pass, rebuilt WASM/browser
artifact, independent STEP matrix or green remote CI. The architecture audit
is a separate publication gate and its baseline is not weakened.
