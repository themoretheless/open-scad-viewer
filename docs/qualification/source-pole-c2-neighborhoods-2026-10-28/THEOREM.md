# Owned pole C2 graph neighborhoods

The native factory borrows a private same-body boundary certificate. Its pole
stratum must contain an owned graph atlas and exact common quadratic jets.
Every edge of each complete native pole vertex link must also retain an exact
full-carrier G2 token. Declared C0 edges and tolerance-only tokens do not suffice.
The factory publishes no atlas until all owned stars pass within its work budget.

## Composition

The graph atlas proves that the original incident source collars form a single
world graph over an open projected disk of a positive reported radius. Its
native link covers every incident natural meridian; the certificate excludes
unmatched boundary tails and the far collar boundary from that disk. This is a
local source-star assertion. Other portions of the body may project into the
same disk.

Each original positive-weight Bezier chart is analytic away from the collapsed
pole. The private transverse bounds make it a smooth graph there. Exact G2 on
all glued meridians identifies the first and second world graph derivatives on
each interface. Finite piecewise gluing therefore gives a C2 graph on the
punctured disk, including those interfaces.

The private common quadratic jets and first-order normal factorizations give
uniform facewise limits for the gradient g and Hessian H at the pole. The
projected radial coercivity implies that these limits hold as the world graph
coordinates tend to the pole. There are finitely many incident charts, and
interface derivatives agree, so the limits are common across the whole disk.
Writing the pole as zero, integration along radial segments gives

    g(x) - g(0) = integral_0^1 H(t*x) * x dt.

Subtracting H(0)*x leaves o(|x|). Thus g is differentiable at the pole with
derivative H(0), and H is continuous there. The local owned world graph is C2.
This does not assert that the original collapsed polar parameterization is
regular at the pole.

## Limits

This construction does not certify whole-body C2, arbitrary ideal sweep laws,
arbitrary authored frames and caps, C3, or arbitrary closed seams. Sharp rims
retain C0. A source geometry refusal remains a refusal: it cannot be replaced
by an independently authored ideal shape or caller-provided smoothness flag.
Diagnostic floating enclosures do not establish exact jet equality; private
original-source exact predicates do.
