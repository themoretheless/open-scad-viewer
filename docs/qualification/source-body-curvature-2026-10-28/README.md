# Original-carrier curvature and mixed seam collection

Rust now compares oriented unit normals and signed world-coordinate curvature
tensors over original UV rectangles with one shared shape-span ledger. The
comparison alone carries no positional authority.

`source_seam_tangency::qualify_curvature` obtains positional ownership and
canonical correspondence only from sealed, privately admitted original carriers.
It retains original pcurves, surfaces, root enclosures, reversed clocks, affine
carrier ranges and rational projective inverse maps. It differentiates original
surfaces before restriction, bounds complete interval cells, and requires one
coherent sign for both the normal and tensor along the entire retained seam.
Endpoint samples cannot grant admission. Thresholds are explicit tolerance-based
G2, not an exact symbolic smoothness assertion.

`qualify_collection` accepts explicit C0, tangent-plane and curvature requests for
every ordinary edge of one immutable admitted original body. It binds the
certificate to that actual body, retains separate G1 and G2 evidence and charges
both methods against the same canonical-cell, pcurve-span and source-surface-cell
budgets. A failed edge or exhausted allowance returns no partial certificate.
Curvature requests retain their own normal-distance and curvature-distance
thresholds; `max_sine_squared` applies to tangent-plane requests only.

## Native qualification scope

The finite checks include nonzero curvature with opposite chart orientation,
G1 with a curvature discontinuity, equal endpoint curvatures with an interior
jump, incompatible normal/tensor orientation signs, each insufficient budget, singular normals, four constant affine world maps with
two independent positive homogeneous weight scalings each, rational root endpoints,
reversed clocks, affine partial carriers and root-ended projective inverse maps
before and after fresh definition restoration. A privately admitted cylinder
checks mixed G1/G2 coverage, all four curved wall joins, eight explicit C0 cap
joins and one-short aggregate budgets. Original root-ended shell admission and
pole refusals are rerun. `verification.json` records actual completed commands,
source hashes and compressed logs, not just the intended matrix.

## Remaining full-goal requirements

This milestone does not close the universal ideal loft/sweep source-law proof,
full frame/cap acceptance matrix, face-interior regularity, or tangent/curvature
limits at pole or vertex strata. Collapsed poles still explicitly refuse this
collection. Supporting charts without proved C2 basis continuity or regular
normal bounds remain unresolved. There is no geometric admission from caller
flags, sampled equality, cropped proxy surfaces or omitted cells. WASM/browser,
STEP delivery and the complete semantic G1 release matrix remain separate.
No TypeScript geometry or transport changes are included.
