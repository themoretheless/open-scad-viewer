The exact midpoint is enclosed by outward arithmetic; an adaptive left-side candidate must have a proved orientation and winding one in the walk. A convex rectangle containing that exact midpoint and the candidate must be proved separated from every other graph edge. This supplies a locally separated interior witness rather than a vertex-average guess. Walk edges must exist in the supplied graph.
