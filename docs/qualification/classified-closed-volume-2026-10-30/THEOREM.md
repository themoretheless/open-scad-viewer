## Successful opaque owner

`OriginalClassifiedVolume` is constructed only by a fresh native computation on
one immutable `MultiSweep`, two immutable original carrier surfaces and an exact
source profile partition. Its private fields retain those exact borrows and the
declared per-span join classes. A report cannot construct or rebind the owner.

The constructor proves, in order:

1. The existing complete original filled-map atlas proves global injectivity
   (with the closed traversal quotient where applicable), regularity and
   preservation of the parameter topology over the complete carrier domain.
2. Both complete original cap maps have the requested C1/C2 continuity, including
   original internal knot lines; unresolved continuity refuses.
3. Original translation, station laws and the actual selected frame are C1/C2.
   Closed traversal uses their periodic original jets and the positive arc clock
   where required. No profile class can bypass this stage.
4. Every source curve is freshly regular and exactly decomposed into its complete
   original Bezier span packet. Every forward join, including all internal knots
   and every ring closure, consumes exactly one declared class. C0 still requires
   exact position equality; G1/G2 additionally require the existing exact rational
   geometric endpoint jet predicate with positive local orientation.

The cap/motion/atlas and profile stages share one aggregate cells/exact-work/
products ledger. Profile visits and joins also share one steps budget. A failed
stage returns diagnostics, never a successful owner. Arithmetic of the existing
motion and profile predicates is unchanged by extracting the shared private
stages.

## Distinct authority and explicit remaining obligations

The legacy `OriginalSmoothVolume` factory still freshly requires uniform profile
Gk on a closed traversal. It cannot return the new classified owner as a legacy
success. In the classified report the closed motion stage deliberately leaves
`profiles_certified` and `differential_compatibility_certified` false. The separate
profile report records the actual mixed classes; declared C0 never gains G1/G2.

This native owner does not prove that those profiles bound an actual material
region on the carriers. In particular a second profile ring is not, by itself,
evidence of a hole, nesting, orientation or valid trims. A subsequent BRep source
owner must freshly prove exact profile/UV carrier binding and valid complete
material trims on these same original inputs before issuing material-boundary
Gk or shell topology authority. The existing closed BRep owner has not yet been
connected to this classified volume API. No retained Solid, cap-wall smoothness,
WASM, browser, STEP or remote CI qualification follows from this packet.

Genuinely differing carrier registrations, contact/polar regimes and other
unresolved global atlas conditions retain their existing refusals; this change
does not make those proof families general.
