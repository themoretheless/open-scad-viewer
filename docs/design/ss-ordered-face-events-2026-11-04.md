# Exact canonical ordering of retained SS/face events

`QualifiedOrderedSsFaceEvents` groups every record of a complete trimmed-face
census by its exact parameter on the SAME owned SS graph. Identical qualified
polynomial/isolator definitions are equal directly; otherwise exact gcd/Sturm
common-root comparison proves equality. Overlapping boxes or matching binary64
coordinates do not weld events. Distinct source parameters remain separate even
if their world images coincide; cross-chart/global vertex identity is more work.

Exact comparisons and rational clock refinement establish strict source-parameter
order. After canonical owner selection, adjacent closed parameter bounds must
be strictly separated. Intersections only tighten proven query enclosures and
never replace original clock definitions. Exhausted order/equality/refinement
budgets return an error instead of a partial order or a midpoint decision.

Every original span/candidate record survives in exactly one event's alias list.
An event is retained when ANY alias is retained by the complete face census;
its canonical record is then chosen from those retained aliases. Local span or
trim exclusions are not allowed to erase a valid representation in another
span. Boundary evidence remains per-record; no material-side/crossing sign is
inferred. Snapshot and original point owners remain reachable through the source.

`OriginalSsRootPoint::with_query_policy` changes algebraic query allowances while
keeping the literal graph and clock owners. `bind_port` uses this to produce
refined original SS endpoint ports without changing `same_selector` identity.
The ordinary native SS edge constructor can consume these ports. Such later
queries have their own allowances outside the completed ordering ledger.

Comparison, clock-query, conservative reserved-work, refinement and event-count
limits are explicit. Replay rebuilds source face/native coverage and every exact
group/order/alias decision. Qualification merges repeated Cox records, preserves
hole exclusions and all alias indices, and constructs/replays an actual original
SS edge between ordered events. A bent two-span NURBS face supplies DIFFERENT
elimination polynomials sharing one tangent seam root: their identity is proved
through the exact common-root path. Resource failures never emit partial events.

Remaining work includes cross-source physical junctions, canonical global
seam/pole/singular identity, ordered face arrangements and actual face splitting,
general algebraic UV/boundary contact proofs, full material trim and intersecting
Boolean/healing. Persistent split/merge lineage/history, actual bridge/WASM calls
and the wider quad/offset/fillet/G2 requirements remain open.
