## Composition proof

For the original reference-profile anchor `a`, the normal-axis fit is

`f(t) = ||G(t) - P(t)|| / (scale(t) * axis_x(t) * a + center_x(t))`.

Path and guide use the constructor's selected normalized station. Scalar and
affine laws keep their authored normalized law clock. The actual filled-map
owner already encloses this same formula; no retained section or rounded
cached contact point supplies a premise.

The motion owner first proves Ck of the original translation, scale/affine
laws and guided frame. Full guided-frame nondegeneracy implies a nonzero
normal projection of `G-P`, hence a positive full offset norm. The additional
contact gate independently proves Ck of guide position, recomputes the anchor
from its original profile and proves a strictly positive denominator on a
complete subdivision cover of `[0,1]`. Norm and division then preserve Ck.
The source caps and previously proved embedded filled map remain mandatory.

The closed owner additionally requires exact periodic source jets. In arc
units, regular source position with forward unit-tangent jets through order
`k-1` gives Ck position via `X_s=L*T`. This permits a regular rational clock
change whose raw endpoint derivatives differ. Every original knot and the
closed seam are checked; a failed Cartesian attempt still consumes work.

The contact gate shares the caller's remaining cell/exact-work ledgers.
Every denominator box must pass; an uncovered box, singular denominator,
precision exhaustion or depleted budget refuses. No primitive or aggregate
work ceiling was raised. On a closed fit failure `laws_certified` is false,
so the public boundary API cannot bypass the fit using frame smoothness alone.

not establish continuous rail coincidence, source trim ownership, a retained
surface join, or cap/wall G1/G2. Those require their existing independent
owners. Classified source corners remain explicitly C0. The BRep constructor
still requires its full retained boundary/material proof, original trim
correspondence and source embedding before exposing a Solid.

guides, distinct authored domains, different nonplanar endpoint caps, C1 vs
