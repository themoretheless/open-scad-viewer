# CST second area moments

`nurbs_core::construct::curves::airfoil::cst_section_moments(&AlgebraicCst, max_work)` encloses second moments of the **original source CST section**, in its canonical XY chord frame. The immutable `AlgebraicCst` retains that source independently of its rounded emitted NURBS. This API uses neither sampled quadrature nor polygon integration of the emitted poles.

`about_origin` and `about_centroid` each store three outward intervals in order `[Ixx, Iyy, Ixy]`, where `Ixx = integral(y² dA)`, `Iyy = integral(x² dA)`, and `Ixy = integral(xy dA)`. The product uses the positive mathematical convention; a mechanical inertia tensor would negate off-diagonal products. Dimensions are length^4. Density, solid mass, spanwise variation, arbitrary world frames, principal axes and aerodynamic properties are outside this contract.

Input is the existing direct algebraic subset: positive integer or half-integer leading exponent, positive integer trailing exponent and degree at most25 after `x=t^power`, power1 or2. Section admission conservatively requires upper shape coefficients >= corresponding lower coefficients, with at least one strict inequality. Cancellation that establishes ordering only after polynomial reduction is not admitted. The inherited construction degree, weight, coordinate and source validation limits remain unchanged.

With normalized upper/lower ordinates `u,l`, form `h=u-l`, `s=u+l`. Then `u²-l²=h*s` and `u³-l³=h*(h²+3*s²)/4`. Bernstein products retain interval coefficients; the cubic product can reach degree75 even though the NURBS construction remains degree25. Binomials at these degrees are enclosed by interval multiplication/division recurrences instead of converting rounded large integers into exact coefficients or overflowing u64. Weighted Bernstein integrals use exact integer products at degree<=75 and exponent<=5, followed by outward interval division. First moments and area are calculated within this same call to center by the parallel-axis theorem; no previously rounded centroid is accepted as authority. Common chord and substitution factors cancel symbolically in the centering correction before scaling by chord^4.

A single work ledger charges interval primitives and combinatorial recurrence steps before execution, within caller budget1..=1,000,000. It is never reset per product or phase. Resource exhaustion, nonfinite interval operations, unresolved positive area and physical fourth-power scale underflow return an error, with no partial certificate. The output may have a negative lower endpoint on a nonnegative central moment because of interval dependency; it must not be interpreted as a negative physical inertia. There is no promised relative error bound. Validation, bounded allocations and source reads are outside the arithmetic ledger, as in the source CST constructor.

Tests compare interval endpoints to independent exact rational beta integrals for symmetric, cambered, reflected, integer-exponent and full-degree nonconstant shapes. They verify chord^4 scaling, signed central products, tight work boundaries, invalid budgets, collapsed/inverted sections and physical-scale underflow. Default and no-codec execution are recorded separately. Native completion supplies no JSON/WASM/Rush/editor/STEP qualification or B-rep/volume authority.
