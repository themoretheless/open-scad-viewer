# Source-qualified paired root boundary sewing

`QualifiedInteriorSsCensus::sew_paired_regions` selects one finite arrangement
region on each support and sews their exact root boundaries into a fresh mixed
model. Each world edge is deduplicated only by literal selectors and owned
original-graph definition. Its two uses must belong to different supports and
require one consistent shell-use reversal. All pcurve world indices are remapped;
the original root curves and original surfaces remain the definitions.

Rational rims and inherited original holes are explicitly refused by this gate.
They require cross-source endpoint/splitting/stitching evidence. Different
boundary component counts, unmatched/repeated edges, impossible signatures,
inconsistent orientation and invalid tolerance cannot publish a boundary owner.

## Why this is an embedded boundary certificate

Each selected patch is an injective original source image of a bounded Jordan
region minus its immediate disjoint child regions. The root-only restriction
excludes unstitched authored rims/holes. Every original cross-support contact
belongs to the complete source census. An arrangement region contains no census
component in its interior; its only contacts are its boundary components. Exact
paired edge incidence therefore accounts for every identification between the
two patches. Their quotient along these common circles is a compact connected
orientable surface, and its map into world space is continuous and one-to-one.
Compactness and Hausdorff world space make it an embedding. Doubling a planar
region with h holes has genus h. Numerical coincidence is never an identification.

The immutable owner retains its source census and region signatures. Replay
requalifies the original census, reconstructs arrangements and repeats sewing.
`boundary_embedded()` is true; `material_admitted()` is false. There is no
conversion to legacy `QualifiedClosedSolid` or an outward material certificate.
Shell orientation is coherent but its global outward sign still needs proof.

effective uses and replay the annular boundary. A reversed original U clock on
the second support exercises the other shell-orientation branch after an
independent original-body admission. Cases with authored holes must refuse
root-only sewing rather than silently filling them.
