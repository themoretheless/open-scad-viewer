# Complete original third-surface boundary census for a chord

`QualifiedRootChordBoundary` audits every outer/hole coedge of the source face against every nonempty tensor Cox span of the chord's other original surface. Each pair has a complete sealed real branch census, not a finite seed search. Excluded roots retain their original exclusion authority. Actual third-surface points whose clock boxes cannot be strictly separated from the finite boundary edge must exactly equal a chord endpoint, then pass original-coordinate endpoint binding and the common-face UV joint proof.

This establishes a sufficient exclusion of additional boundary contact: any point on the inserted chord is on its other surface, whose contacts with the face boundary have all been accounted for. Extra surface contacts outside the finite chord image may conservatively cause refusal. Unknown identity, algebraic lifts, root coverage or budgets never authorize absence.

Finite edge ranges use the original chart clocks. Projection/span/root/identity budgets are explicit and bounded. The certificate retains every branch census and exposes audit counts. Replay checks the split source and regenerates complete coverage.

This does not establish that the chord lies inside the retained face, is embedded, or selects correct material. Jordan/region nesting, singular/tangent/periodic/pole completeness, healing and general intersecting Boolean remain required.

Regression includes successful complete coverage of the ten coedge uses of a circle split at two algebraic endpoints, exact endpoint/UV authorization, refusal of incomplete census budgets, and refusal when the inserted chord crosses an additional hole boundary. That negative case asserts refusal only: unresolved rational-univariate lifting is also a valid reason for refusal and is not promoted into a general intersection-detection claim.
