# Complete finite third-span classification on one owned SS graph

`QualifiedSsThirdSurfaceBranchCensus` covers the entire CLOSED parameter domain
of one owned original deflated SS graph against one finite third NURBS span.
Source surfaces, original Cox spans, free axis and the graph's unit-span domain
must match even when the result is empty. Exact third-surface elimination supplies
necessary scalar constraints; the complete exact scalar census retains every
distinct real candidate in that graph interval, including endpoints.

Every candidate receives its own ordinary exact SS point owner and either a
positive original-equation membership certificate or an independent exclusion
certificate. Outside entries remain in the ordered coverage list. An unresolved
candidate aborts qualification; there is no partial/unresolved entry in a complete
certificate. Resource/numeric failures propagate. Only an input-level absence of
an exclusion proof permits the separately budgeted positive membership attempt.
Failed positive lifting never becomes an outside result.

Any actual third-span member on the graph satisfies the localized necessary
polynomial and therefore occurs in the complete scalar census. Original graph
uniqueness identifies the owned point at that parameter. A sealed inside/outside
proof for EVERY candidate establishes full coverage for this graph/span query.
This does not identify duplicate world points across other charts or prove a
whole-body arrangement, trimmed-face membership or material selection.

Clock binding and both possible point queries consume explicit query slots.
Before each attempt its full clock/algebra allowance is reserved; failed
attempts keep their reservation. The scalar census allowance is reserved before
root isolation. Defaults are 192 point-query slots and 16,000,000 reserved work
units, with separate scalar and per-point policies. The bound is conservative,
not a measured CPU-operation count. Existing candidate/curve construction and
reverification are outside this constructor reservation. No root or query is
silently skipped when a local or aggregate allowance is exhausted.

Replay rebuilds original source/graph/scalar proofs and the complete classification,
checking each stored point's literal graph/candidate owners, clock definition,
evidence, coverage size and reservation trace. Qualification covers all-inside,
mixed and all-outside lists; wrong SS branches; a true empty disjoint result with
axis checks; unresolved algebraic lifts; root/query/reservation/point-work limits.

Remaining work includes algebraic UV extensions and nonisolated contacts,
trimmed-face holes and periodic/pole arrangements, cross-source junctions,
complete physical branch assembly and winding, full trim/material/Boolean/healing,
lineage/history, actual bridge/WASM calls and the wider quad/offset/fillet/G2 scope.
