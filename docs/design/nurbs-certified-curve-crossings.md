# Certified curve crossings in Rust

`nurbs_core::intersection::intersect_curve_curve_certified_report` is separate from the legacy numerical-candidate API. It admits exact checked affine spatial line pairs, original positive-rational curves on an exactly shared axis-aligned plane, and literal same-definition coincidence. Other spatial curves remain explicitly unresolved rather than acquiring a false existence certificate.

For affine clamped two-pole constant-weight 3D lines, every subtraction, determinant product, quotient identity and reconstructed coordinate is checked for exact binary64 representability. A nonparallel two-coordinate system has a unique candidate; equality on the third coordinate proves its actual 3D incidence. An inconsistent third coordinate or an out-of-segment exact parameter proves emptiness. Any failed exact arithmetic check disables the specialization. Natural-domain parameter mapping is outward; clamped endpoint contacts are explicitly Boundary.

For planar sources, equality of every original pole's fixed coordinate proves exact common-plane containment. The product of original active knot spans is covered. Original rational hull separation excludes cells. A Krawczyk self-inclusion and an infinity-norm contraction of the complete original Jacobian prove existence and uniqueness of a transverse root. Midpoint residuals are outward original-curve enclosures, not rounded numerical values relabeled exact. The numerical inverse only proposes a preconditioner; its arithmetic accuracy is not trusted as a proof. Accepted spatial boxes meet the supplied localization tolerance, which never relaxes the exact intersection equation.

When existence is proved but localization is insufficient, every parent root lies in its Krawczyk image, so refinement preserves that image instead of cutting the root onto a new subdivision boundary. Otherwise bounded subdivision covers the whole original parameter product. Exhausted or unsupported cells retain full parameter obligations. Distinct admitted interior cells have unique roots; roots on original/internal subdivision boundaries can remain unresolved. Tangencies, other coincident parameterizations, singular contacts and general oblique nonlinearly spatial curves are not silently classified.

Limits: validated nonperiodic original 3D curves, positive finite spatial tolerance, 1..100000 visited cells, at most 4096 initial active span pairs and subdivision depth 64. Literal equality and checked affine-line specialization have bounded fixed work outside the adaptive cell count. `complete` describes the supplied source domains; `identical_parameter_overlap` is exact source-definition equality, not phase or image-coincidence inference. No B-rep trim/Boolean authority is granted. Later floating-point curve evaluation is separate from the exact nominal input-definition enclosure.

Six independent integration tests cover nonunit domains, oblique 3D crossings and skew projected crossings, rational parameterization, two curved roots with complete coverage, hull separation, literal overlap, tangency, endpoints, unsupported spatial curves, invalid limits and resource obligations. The full core unit suite passes with 1342 tests. Adapter/export qualification remains separate.
