representation on one specified finite third NURBS span. It is independent of
the positive membership qualifier: a failed lift, exhausted work or unresolved
bound does not produce an exclusion.

Two sufficient witnesses are supported. First, the necessary exact elimination
polynomial is reduced modulo the owned clock and enclosed on its exact selected
interval; separation from zero contradicts any triple-surface solution. Second,
a linear coordinate relation from the ORIGINAL localized elimination basis
forces a rational coordinate value at that clock. Its denominator must be
separated from zero, and its entire exact image must be strictly disjoint from
the original SS graph domain or the third unit Cox span. All hypothetical real
triple solutions satisfy this necessary basis relation. This distinguishes the
negative proof from merely proposing rational maps for a positive lift.

Source surfaces, spans, free axis and original deflated graph authority must
match. The entire source graph must lie within its real unit Cox spans, where
the original positive-weight denominators are nonzero. Every valid finite third
point can therefore be lifted into the localized system. Closed allowed bounds
are retained; touching a boundary is insufficient for exclusion. The result
keeps literal point/candidate owners, witness kind, exact image and allowed
interval, policies and arithmetic work. Replay regenerates the source, point and
witness. Clock and algebra have separate explicit work budgets.

nonroot scalar parameter and a constant disjointness constraint. It keeps a
point exactly on the closed third boundary and excludes a genuine dyadic gap

exclusion; deficient clock or algebra policies return errors.
