# Original-source quadric membership

`nurbs_core::analysis::implicit_quadric::inspect(surface, Q, max_exact_work, max_cells)` checks membership in the caller's literal symmetric equation `[x,y,z,1]^T Q [x,y,z,1]=0`, then checks a nonvanishing gradient continuously over the represented image. It does not infer a best-fit quadric from data. This is one general capability, not separate counts for sphere, cylinder, ellipsoid and graph quadrics. The initial checkpoint added public qualification to the existing implementation; the multispan checkpoint extends its original-source proof.

Admission is a nonperiodic clamped tensor B-spline of degree 1..8 per axis, positive finite weights, three coordinates per control, and existing control/1e9 coordinate/weight/Q bounds. Periodic and unclamped storage return an unproved result. Single Bezier tensors retain the original exact path; multispan tensors use original knots and a shared-context homogeneous blossom proof. Rounded Cartesian decomposition is never substituted for the original definition.

The native predicate forms exact expansion coefficients of `H^T Q H`, where `H=(w*x,w*y,w*z,w)`, from original binary64 leaves. No samples or common coordinate/matrix normalization establish membership. Any nonzero residual, even below a sampling or underflow threshold, prevents exact membership. `inspect_with_constant_products` retains up to sixteen literal products as additions to the homogeneous constant term. `inspect_sphere` uses these products to retain exact original center squares and radius squared, rather than passing a rounded squared radius as a new literal equation. Even a geometrically intended sphere built with rounded transforms can fail exact membership; no tolerance snaps it into the supplied equation.

Exact membership and regular implicit support are distinct. After exact membership, outward interval evaluation covers the whole patch domain and bounds a nonzero component of the implicit gradient on every accepted cell. The report exposes exact work, cell charges, a positive gradient lower bound on success, and refusal reasons. Zero exact work or too few cells cannot inherit a prior positive result. Existing requests up to 16,000,000 exact-work units are accepted, but the actual predicate context remains capped at `cad_predicates::MAX_WORK` (1,000,000). The interval-cell ceiling remains 100,000. Neither ceiling is increased here. These are separate ledgers, not one combined computational-work bound.

A cone patch including its singular apex may have exact membership while continuous regular support is unproved. Conversely, a collapsed parameter patch contained in an ordinary plane may have regular ambient implicit support. That does not establish two-dimensional parameter rank, injectivity, complete coverage, a pole quotient, orientation, trimming, shell ownership, material or a valid solid. Consumers must discharge those obligations independently. Reports are fresh inspections of input, not reusable caller-supplied authority.

Public contracts exercise saddle and elliptic paraboloid graphs, rational cylinder and ellipsoid patches, literal radius-product retention, weight-gauge scaling, all resource boundaries, one-ULP control/weight/equation mutations, singular and collapsed cases, multispan refusal and invalid inputs. This native contract does not qualify WASM, Rush, editor or STEP use.


For each nonempty knot rectangle, de Boor blossom denominators are cleared in expansion arithmetic. U extraction batches every local V control and homogeneous component; V extraction then batches all resulting U components. Common normalization includes every active blossom/channel, retaining one common positive homogeneous gauge per stage. Independently normalizing rows would alter the tensor and is forbidden. The exact polynomial is tested on every cell before any regular ambient support is admitted. One ULP in a last-span control or weight cannot inherit first-span equality. Native source arenas bind both original knot vectors, degrees and tensor dimensions. The outward gradient evaluator unions enclosures from all overlapping source knot cells.

The multispan public fixtures include degree1×1 saddles, degree2×1 and transposed degree1×2 rational cylinders with nonuniform other-axis weights, degree2×2 rational sphere chains and degree8×1 quadratic graphs authored with exact integer Bernstein controls. Nonuniform dyadic knot sources exercise non-dyadic extraction denominators without rounding the proof. Existing resource bounds remain unchanged; inconclusive arithmetic or budget exhaustion refuses proof rather than fitting a replacement.
