# Original-boundary nonlinear correspondence

Native Rust partial qualification of NURBS-0197. This does not change its planned status or the 184/200 total.

`nurbs_core::curve_surface_agreement::verify_correspondence` checks an authored monotone piecewise affine relation between normalized original 3D-edge and original 2D-pcurve traversal parameters. `verify_oriented_correspondence` additionally reverses the original edge traversal through outward interval arithmetic. Both coordinates may increase or decrease, with a fixed sense. Partial ranges are allowed by the core and explicitly specified by the first/last nodes; success for a partial range does not cover omitted geometry.

The verifier evaluates homogeneous blossoms of the original rational curves with interval endpoint mapping, then bounds the original surface composition using correlated Bernstein coefficients. Unsupported span/degree layouts fall back to original curve and periodic surface chart image boxes. Each accepted cell covers its entire affine relation. Midpoint image separation rejects the supplied relation only. Budget or precision exhaustion retains the complete requested relation as an unresolved obligation and returns no distance upper certificate. Rounded curve extraction, samples and display meshes never admit a relation.

Inputs require validated positive-weight geometry, 2..1025 finite nodes in [0,1], strict monotonicity in both coordinates, positive finite tolerance and 1..100000 cells. The supplied binary64 fractions define exact nominal parameters; affine mapping, reversal and composition arithmetic are enclosed. Kernel evaluation rounding at later user queries is separate. `distance_upper` is present only after all segments pass. Cells count visited proof cells, not arithmetic operations.

`brep_core::watertight::inspect_boundary_correspondence` accepts two distinct authored models with equal tolerances and free nondegenerate boundaries. Its relation covers both entire coedge traversals and opposes their face orientations. It independently verifies the common original source edge against its source face lift and the target face lift, using a shared 1..1000000-cell budget and at most 100000 cells per lift. Thus both lifts are within model tolerance of a common edge, and their mutual distance is at most twice that tolerance. Each report remains available. Other faces/edges of either model are not globally audited by this selected-boundary API.

`infer_boundary_correspondence` proposes 1..1024 map segments by a bounded nearest-lift search (129 coarse samples and 40 ternary iterations per interior node). Search is numerical and may miss a branch or local minimum. It never grants admission: only the independent original-domain verifier can return `within_tolerance`. Nonmonotone proposals remain unqualified; failure is not evidence of universal geometric separation. Whole-boundary endpoints are fixed, and no closed-seam phase search is claimed. Inputs are immutable, and no topology is authored.

Still open for NURBS-0197: automatic fragmentation of partial overlaps; ownership and uniqueness across a complete patch set; integration of nonlinear matches with all-boundary coverage; closed-seam phases and degeneracies. Seam closeness alone does not prove trim simplicity, manifold embedding, orientation, volume or a watertight solid. Existing affine patch-set matching and explicit rounded boundary-portion authoring retain their original contracts.

Qualification logs and source fingerprints: [native tests](../qualification/nurbs-boundary-correspondence-2026-10-15/native-tests.json). Scoped correspondence tests pass; the complete B-rep gate still has real general-SS Boolean refusals and is not marked passed.
