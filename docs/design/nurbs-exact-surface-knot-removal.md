# Exact tensor-surface knot removal

`nurbs_core::editing::exact_surface_knot_removal::remove(source, axis, knot, max_work)` removes one existing interior knot in U or V only when exact original-parameter equality is established for the actual emitted binary64 surface definition. It keeps the complete absolute UV domain, traversal, degrees, unedited tensor basis and its periodic flag/storage. The edited axis must be clamped and nonperiodic. Existing Surface limits stay unchanged: degree1..25 and at most256 controls per axis, three coordinates, finite positive conditioned weights and bounded coordinates/knots. Invalid knot, storage, net or budget returns an input error.

Each original row along the selected axis first uses the exact curve removal proposal and complete rational parameter identity. That alone is insufficient: an identical rational row may have a different homogeneous gauge varying with the edited parameter. Tensor blending such rows can then change the surface between them. A second independent exact polynomial identity compares the **stored-weight denominator functions**, encoding each scalar polynomial in the existing exact restriction predicate with Cartesian controls `[weight,0,0]` and rational weights1. These are literal source/output weights, never rounded products of Cartesian coordinates and weights, and are not subject to the Curve Cartesian-coordinate limit because they are not constructed geometry. Complete same-domain equality of each Cartesian row plus its positive denominator implies equality of the homogeneous row. The unedited tensor basis combines those identical homogeneous functions, preserving the complete nominal surface.

All curve proposals, rational identity proofs and denominator identity proofs draw from one caller ledger in1..=1,000,000; no row, axis phase or predicate receives a fresh full budget. Copies, source arena assembly and bounded input/output validation remain outside that arithmetic ledger. `rows_verified` counts only rows which passed both checks. A failed row, indeterminate proof, resource exhaustion or invalid final net returns the entire unchanged input surface, never a partly edited net. Resource refusal is inconclusive about other candidate representations. One numeric inverse-insertion proposal per row is not an exhaustive minimality search.

Accepted output preserves nominal real-valued geometry and UV parameterization. Binary64 evaluator rounding may differ. No topology IDs, seam quotient certificate, embedding, regularity, material/volume or B-rep history is carried across a changed definition. Source-bound downstream certificates must be rebuilt. JSON, WASM, Rush, editor and exchange integration are separate.

Independent tests cover both axes on a nonunit UV polynomial, rational equations and large allowed literal weights, an adversarial constant-row surface whose row curves are unchanged but whose denominator would change the tensor, late-row perturbations and atomic refusal, whole-operation budget boundaries, preserved other-axis periodic storage, edited-axis periodic refusal and bounded degree25 exhaustion. The adversarial surface at its middle evaluates to y=1/3; accepting only per-row rational identity would incorrectly change it to y=1/2.

## Bounded surface simplification pass

`simplify(source, axis, max_work)` visits each original interior knot occurrence once, including multiplicities, sharing the same arithmetic ledger across every attempted removal and all its row/denominator proofs. A successful step is committed; refusal leaves that step unchanged while retaining previously proved steps. The returned `attempts` retains each knot and refusal, `removed` counts committed removals, and `completed_pass` distinguishes a full visit from resource interruption. Completing a pass does not prove minimality or that a different proposal could not succeed. An axis without interior knots completes with zero work. The selected axis must remain clamped and nonperiodic, including for an empty pass. Other-axis periodic storage remains untouched. This extends NURBS-0226 and does not create another catalogue item.
