# Original sweep guarantees and product delivery gates

This audit separates the original ideal-body theorem from retained BRep
validity and from product exposure. No layer may infer the former merely
because the latter passed. All geometric decisions belong to Rust.

The `brep_sweep_boundary_construct` native entry owns original cap surfaces,
material trims, error bounds and an optional original-volume proof. Its atlas
can certify global original embedding, volume regularity and preservation of
parameter topology, with typed refusal under unproved hypotheses or exhausted
shared work. The different-carrier extension has a fresh guarded native,
private WASM and independent STEP matrix. A browser bridge check separately
covers the six-face public original-carrier request, STEP and budget refusals.
Its physical cap joins explicitly remain C0.

Rush progressive sweep construction and Solid admission are another route.
`geometry-bridge/src/sweep_pipeline.rs` owns the transferred model admission;
`replay_admission.rs` binds native replay and actual model identity.
The TS admission facade forwards the exact native artifact to Rust. The
existing four-case viewport/Solid lifecycle matrix validates that route.
It does not establish that a Rush document exposes the new arbitrary authored
source-cap atlas request or its original ideal-volume flags. Those are
separate API and integration obligations; raw JSON/browser success is not
Rush/viewport evidence for that new source class.

Before claiming full delivery, Rust must bind the original source-cap/material
map and actual final model to the Rush construction/replay route, preserve
independent original/retained G1/G2 scope and explicit C0, and refuse missing
source ownership. Fresh Rush, viewport and Solid cases must exercise the new
binding, including cancellation, source replacement, restoration and refusal.
No geometric correction, certificate reconstruction or admission algorithm is
to be introduced in TypeScript to close this gap.

The general atlas is conditional. Closed nonidentical carrier transitions,
nonplanar physical charts outside the proved sufficient classes, cap/wall G1/G2
and mixed seams require their own original-source premises and complete shared
ledgers. Existing positive cases do not constitute universal acceptance or a
full qualification of every combination. The complete semantic G1 matrix,
public artifact/source bindings and hosted CI remain independent gates.
