# Original rational frames and filled annular caps

The new typed Rust owner accepts literal rational Bezier affine pose laws,
original world profiles, a fixed rational carrier and its original UV material
trims. It does not interpolate sampled frames. Motion degrees 1 through 8 and
positive original weights are bounded and validated before proof budgets.
Original one-sided time derivatives use homogeneous coefficient numerators.

The whole original map is F(u,v,t)=T(t)+A(t)(Q(u,v)-origin). The exhaustive
native atlas must prove every diagonal and distinct parameter pair on UV x S1.
A local Jacobian sign or a regular constructed BRep supplies no global credit.
Literal full-pose station and closure equality establish the quotient charts.
Valid original UV material regions then retain their topology under the proved
embedding, including their holes. Unknown conditions and exhausted budgets
return no certified Solid.

A single optional native affine coordinate proposal is independently checked
for invertibility using outward arithmetic. It is applied to original pose
coefficients as interval linear combinations before rational evaluation and
carrier multiplication. Rounded transformed poles never replace the source.
The complete transformed atlas still proves all pairs. Invertibility transports
embedding and regularity back to world coordinates; orientation is separately
queried from the original world Jacobian. All attempts share the explicit ledger.
The report retains the coordinate rows, offset and determinant enclosure.
An optional exact original column identity A_j=alpha*A_i+C factors the shared
carrier coordinate before multiplication. Every raw control coefficient must
satisfy the identity with error-free product/subtraction checks, charged to the
same exact-work budget. This is valid for the entire rational law regardless of
its positive weights. A one-ULP mismatch rejects the factor; no near-equality,
underflowed FMA residual or rounded source replacement is accepted.

Every actual boundary face must exactly equal its original profile/time tensor
patch, including rational weight products, domains, signed chart gauge and
one-to-one face coverage. Exact arithmetic checks refuse inexact construction.
Every actual edge, vertex link and face is then inspected. Complete actual G1/G2
therefore transfers to the same ideal original boundary without an approximation
error term. This does not transfer smoothness between unrelated source laws.

A physical annular cap here is an entire monotone original-UV graph interval
transported over S1. Scalar native regularity proves its nonzero parameter
speed; a single clamped Bezier basis ensures continuity and endpoint ownership.
The original global embedding proves the transported full annulus is embedded.
Exact face binding locates every chart of every selected cap. Selecting caps
never omits edges from smoothness checks. Planar and nonplanar caps are supported
by this representation; source UV bounds make no claim of world height/radius.

The rounded washer fixtures use exact dyadic meridian profiles and rational
angular frame coefficients with weights [1, 1/2, 1]. That angular law is not an
exact circle. Their real top/bottom caps each contain four complete actual faces.
All wall/cap, profile and periodic-time joins are checked. A curvature mutation
can retain G1 while refusing G2. A sharp join requires explicit C0; even an empty
cap selection cannot hide that failed G1 obligation. C0 still requires global
embedding, exact correspondence, closed vertex links and every face's regularity.

This owner does not qualify collapsed pole/disk caps, arbitrary rounded or
transcendental source laws, the older axial MultiSweep physical-cap construction,
or mixed sharp/smooth classification. Those need their own original-source
boundary charts and proofs. New typed native capability also does not imply
JSON/WASM runtime, independent STEP, semantic G1, browser or fresh CI qualification.
