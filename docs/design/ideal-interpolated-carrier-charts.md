# Original interpolated carrier charts

Rust now admits a sufficient open arc-spaced physical tube class with two
different planar rational carriers. Each original carrier must be exactly
transverse to the initial longitudinal axis. Closed sources still require an
identical carrier; no new closed transition or physical cap G1/G2 is claimed.

For normalized UV, C_s=(1-s)C_0+sC_1. Independently proving that C_0 and C_1
are regular embeddings does not prove C_s: opposite endpoint parameter
orientations can collapse the middle section. The owner encloses the complete
original normalized-UV Jacobian hull of both carriers and checks one fixed
coordinate projection and one rounded inverse with outward contraction <1.
Every intermediate Jacobian lies in that same convex hull. This proves
injectivity and rank two for every C_s, including original one-sided knots.
No carrier is sampled, fitted, snapped, or modified to obtain this premise.

Positive uniform and transverse axis laws give an injective regular physical
coordinate change at every station. Time variation of C_s is absorbed by that
change, as are transverse scale and center derivatives. A containing radius
covers both original positive rational control hulls. The existing fresh
original frame/tube proof and exhaustive source-pair atlas then establish
three-dimensional regularity and injectivity. Failed covers and alternative
proof attempts remain charged in the shared work ledger.

BRep owns material trim identity separately. Original UV controls, rational
weights and curve knot phases are compared under positive affine domain
pullbacks using exact predicates on authored binary64 leaves. Equal weight
gauges and equal normalized phases are allowed; one-ULP geometric changes do
not become equal through tolerance. This does not cover arbitrary nonlinear
reparameterizations or changed trim topology.

The public atlas request accepts an explicit aggregate cell budget up to
1,000,000, matching the existing NURBS atlas limit. The non-atlas limit remains
100,000 and every primitive profile regularity query is still capped at
100,000. No default proof budget or geometric tolerance is increased.

Focused tests cover differing rational weights and UV domains, exact/zero/
one-short budgets, an intermediate carrier collapse, unchanged source data,
Solid admission and native STEP round-trip. The six-face public case has
C0 joins and explicitly reports no smoothness certificate. Independent OCCT
checks topology, original NURBS data and volume; full frozen native/WASM,
browser, semantic G1 and CI qualification are separate gates.

Budget regressions for the existing closed source matrices exhaust a required
frame premise. A successful whole proof trace is not a proven minimum: with
a tighter exact budget, skipping an optional failed plane proposal can leave
a sound alternative atlas proof. Such success is not budget exhaustion.
