## Exact geometric source profiles

`cad-predicates::rational_bezier_geometric_endpoint_identity` reads original
positive-weight Bezier controls on both sides. Each array starts at the shared
endpoint and points into its own side. It checks literal rational position
identity and opposite nonzero Cartesian tangent directions. There is no dummy
surface, sampled normal, tolerance, square root or rounded speed ratio.

For homogeneous endpoint jets `H,H1,H2`, with weight jets `W,W1,W2`, use exact
Cartesian derivative numerators:

```
D = H1 W - H W1
E = H2 W² - H W2 W - 2 H1 W W1 + 2 H W1²
```

Cartesian first and second derivatives are `D/W²` and `E/W³`. Choose one
nonzero tangent coordinate `k`. Opposite collinearity gives the positive local
speed ratio `lambda = -Db[k] Wa² / (Da[k] Wb²)`. G2 holds when

```
R = Da[k]² Wb Eb - Db[k]² Wa Ea
R cross Da = 0
```

These identities prove that the second-derivative difference after the speed
change is tangent. All divisions have been eliminated by cross multiplication.
Consequently a nonbinary speed ratio is proved without replacing it by a nearby
floating value. Positive weights and nonzero tangents supply its denominator
and orientation premises. Zero tangents receive no regularity credit.

The NURBS source owner checks spanwise original profile regularity and exact
original-to-Bezier decomposition before using this predicate. Every join,
including internal source knots and each ring closure, is inspected. No selected
seam or caller exclusion list stands for the full set.

## Exact material boundary for source charts

`source_seam_tangency::vertices::material_boundary` combines the material
volume owner, certified original face interiors, exact declared seam jets,
material-side orientation, and complete physical vertex neighborhoods. The
face and seam proofs must refer to the same immutable source body. Regular
and pole-stratified face proofs use the same certificate. The edge and vertex
projections select candidates for the native proofs.

The certificate retains each declared C0/G1/G2 edge and every vertex order.
The global order is the minimum of the face-interior, edge and vertex orders.
A failure or budget exhaustion in any stage leaves the certificate absent and
reports the stage and unresolved entity. This pipeline starts from admitted
original charts. An analytic loft or sweep law still needs a separate proof
that those charts describe its full original material boundary.

Source support assembly resolves the active face-local UV clock of each
authored whole-world coedge before reconstructing a region. Exact knot
restriction and exact traversal reversal preserve the active trace even when
the stored pcurve extends beyond it. The assembly also checks exact face-sense
reflection and charges these operations to its incidence allowance. A partial
world interval remains a diagnostic window under the model contract and
receives no whole-shell certificate. Endpoint lift metadata must match its
authored UV traversal.

The complete closed-sweep audit also checks each face's internal knot lines.
For nonperiodic charts, the spline basis can establish the requested interior
order from knot multiplicity. Exact raw parameter jets can establish
continuity when the basis does not guarantee it. The geometric route tiles
the original clamped tensor chart at every remaining deficient knot line.
The shared controls retain the original gauge, and the native exact strip
proof checks each interface with positive reparameterization and regularity.
Crossing U/V cuts contribute all adjacent tile interfaces. The audit uses the
remaining regularity cells and exact work, and reports the unresolved face
and number of geometric interfaces proved.

For a periodic chart, the audit first opens exactly one authored period along
each periodic axis. It checks all internal interfaces in the same grid and
adds the physical last-to-first interface along each opened axis. A doubly
periodic chart receives both families of wrap interfaces, including their
intersection. Exact opening, tiling and geometric seam identities all draw
from the shared exact work allowance.

When two coedges of one periodic face use the opposite full sides of an
authored period, the audit binds their shared world edge to the exact wrap
proof. Both uses must agree with the same world traversal, including its
orientation. The audit also requires exact positional agreement and valid
vertex links. The report lists these edges separately from its standalone
strip proofs.

The classified BRep audit also accepts paired full uses of opposite sides
of a periodic chart. It opens the original tensor chart exactly and checks
each wrap at the order assigned to that edge. The same coefficient budget
covers exact opening, clock alignment and seam jets. The existing material
and topology proofs remain independent of this edge audit.

The regular original material body also accepts exact geometric continuity
inside its source faces. The face proof compares raw one-sided jets first.
If that comparison gives no certificate, a bounded exact partition can
prove geometric joins across the original knot lines and periodic wraps.
The proof retains the original face and shares the same cell and exact-work
allowances with the raw comparison. Source embedding, differential rank,
edge classifications and physical vertex neighborhoods remain independently
qualified.

Source edge ownership can bind a multispan chart when the canonical world
edge and original pcurve agree for the entire normalized traversal. This
route checks chart inclusion and exact equality. It covers both directions
of the full affine traversal. General projective and partial traversals
continue to require their own exact composition evidence.

An embedded regular face can use exact planarity when its active source
controls span one plane and its internal knot multiplicities ensure position
continuity. A constant coordinate proves the axis-aligned case. For an
oblique plane, the exact rational affine hull supplies the certificate.
Its physical interior neighborhoods lie in that plane even when raw second
derivatives jump. The control census, knot census and affine hull share one
work allowance. Edge classifications remain separate.

An exact pair of adjacent Bézier tiles can also use geometric graph jets
when the constant-scale strip proof is insufficient. The strip must first
prove exact positional equality along the common boundary parameter.
Strict matching signs for a projected Jacobian component give inverse
charts on opposite sides of the complete seam. Exact gradient and Hessian
identities then compare the physical graph in that projection. Normal
enclosures and graph identities consume the remaining seam work.
